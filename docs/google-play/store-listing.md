# Store listing text

**App name (max 30):**
Self Wallet: Expense Tracker

**Short description (max 80):**
Auto-track expenses from bank SMS. Personal & corporate. Private, offline, free.

**Full description (max 4000):**

Self Wallet turns the bank and card SMS you already receive into a clean, automatic expense log – without an account, without ads and without your data ever leaving your phone.

AUTOMATIC FROM SMS
• Debit, credit, card, UPI and ATM messages become transactions instantly – amount, currency, merchant and card
• OTPs, promotions, reminders and failed payments are ignored
• Import past bank SMS to start with history
• Duplicate alert: the same payment SMS twice, or the same charge twice within minutes, so you can check with your bank

PERSONAL & CORPORATE
• One tap: Personal or Corporate – right from the notification
• Your own companies, clients and family as destinations
• Claim tracker: Not claimed → Submitted → Paid back
• PDF expense report with receipts, ready to email; CSV export too

MONEY UNDER CONTROL
• Monthly budgets with alerts at 80% and 100%
• Card bill reminders and subscription renewals
• Balances and card limits read from SMS
• Alerts for unusually large payments
• Split expenses with friends and send reminders

MULTI-CURRENCY & TRAVEL
• 40+ currencies detected automatically, converted with rates you control
• Travel mode tags every expense of a trip and reports it separately

INSIGHTS
• Trends, 6-month chart, spending calendar, categories
• Monthly summary on the 1st
• Home-screen widget and quick add, voice entry and templates

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet permission – the app cannot send your data anywhere
• No sign-up, no ads, no tracking
• Optional backup to a file on your phone, Google Drive or OneDrive that you choose
• App lock with fingerprint/face

Two beautiful styles – Calm Teal and Paper Ledger – each in light and dark.

Free and open source: https://github.com/faisalkazi123-code/Self-Wallet
