# Self Wallet – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

Self Wallet ("the app") is a personal expense tracker that runs entirely on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **your data never leaves your device unless you choose to save a backup file somewhere yourself.**

## What the app accesses

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **SMS messages** (read & receive) | To find bank, card and wallet messages and turn them into expense/income records, bill reminders and account balances. Messages that are not financial (personal chats, OTPs, promotions) are ignored and not stored. | Processed on your phone only. The text of financial messages is stored in the app's private database so you can see where a transaction came from. |
| **Transactions, categories, budgets, trips, notes** you enter | To provide the app's features. | App's private storage on your phone. |
| **Receipt photos** you take or pick | Attached to expenses and included in PDF reports you create. | App's private storage on your phone. |
| **Notifications** permission | To alert you about new transactions, possible duplicate charges, budgets, bills and the monthly summary. | — |
| **Biometric / screen lock** (optional) | To lock the app. Handled by Android; the app never sees your fingerprint or face data. | — |
| **Microphone via the system speech recogniser** (optional, only when you tap the mic) | Voice entry of an expense. Speech is handled by your phone's speech service under its own privacy policy; the app only receives the recognised text. | — |

## What the app does NOT do

- The app has **no internet permission**. It cannot send your data anywhere.
- No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
- Your data is never sold or shared.

## Backups you control

If you choose **Settings › Your data**, the app writes a backup file to a location *you* pick through Android's file picker – for example your phone storage, Google Drive or OneDrive. Uploading to a cloud drive is done by that drive's own app under its own privacy policy. CSV and PDF exports are also only saved where you choose.

## Data retention and deletion

All data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual records in the app, or everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Backup files you created must be deleted by you from where you saved them.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
